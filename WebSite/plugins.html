catalog

Make Lumen yours.

Plugins are single JavaScript files. They load live, unload cleanly, and say which permissions they need. Install one in a click from the Plugins panel, or drop the file in ~/.lumen/plugins.

Made a plugin?

Open a pull request on the registry: once it is reviewed, it shows up here and in every Lumen.

accounts

Publish plugins, themes and wallpapers. Rate what you use.

Browsing and downloading never need an account. Publishing and rating will: the account only exists to keep the catalog free of spam and abuse.

  • Kept: a username, the email you sign in with, and what you publish. Nothing else.
  • Your password is never stored in clear. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold or shared.
  • Delete your account and everything goes with it.
